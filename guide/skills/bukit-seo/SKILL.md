# Bukit SEO

SEO config lives under `site.seo`. `SeoPipeline` builds canonical, alternates,
Open Graph, Twitter, article metadata, JSON-LD, and reports.

Use `bukit seo audit --dir dist` after builds and `bukit seo diff` for report
comparison.
